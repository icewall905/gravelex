GravelEx 0.1.2 adds blood oxygen visibility directly in the app.

- Health tab: SpO2 reading and measurement timestamp, sample count and observed trend for the selected day/week/month. Missing samples remain gaps.
- Health settings: oxygen setup/status, watch switch state, latest stored sample and raw device quality, automatic export/token status, and the last successful upload.
- Upload success time/count survive app restarts and reset when the destination or token changes.

Install GravelEx.apk over your existing GravelEx, or update through Obtainium. The package and permanent signing key are unchanged; watch pairing, history and settings are retained. SpO2 remains experimental spot data rather than continuous monitoring.
